The idea

Engineer's brain, storyteller's heart.

Two koi, one system. The teal fish is the discipline that makes a brand hold together. The terracotta fish is the warmth that makes people care. Every choice in this system balances the two.

The engineer

Systems that hold

Structure, precision, and repeatability. Clear type, tidy spacing, tokens that do not drift. The part of the brand you can measure.

The storyteller

Feeling at scale

Warmth, motion, and a point of view. Copy that moves a room, color that carries emotion. The part of the brand people remember.

Calm Precise Motivational Human-centered Lightly playful Earthy and timeless
Logo suite

The marks

Six approved lockups. Match the mark to its background. The koi carry the brand, so give them room and never redraw them.

Primary horizontal lockupWordmark split around the koi. Default for wide spaces, site headers, decks.
Badge lockup, lightKoi badge beside the wordmark. Use on Soft Sand or white.
Badge lockup, darkReversed wordmark for Oxford Blue and photography.
Koi badge, dark circleOxford Blue disc. Use on light surfaces. Avatars, favicons, stamps.
Koi badge, sand circleSoft Sand disc. Use on dark surfaces where the dark badge would vanish.
Stacked logomarkIKIGAI over creative with the koi tail. For square and tall formats.

Do

  • Keep clear space on all sides equal to the height of the koi badge.
  • Pick the mark whose disc contrasts with the background, dark badge on light, sand badge on dark.
  • Scale the whole lockup as one unit.
  • Reproduce from the master files only.

Do not

  • Recolor the koi or the wordmark, or swap in off-palette colors.
  • Stretch, rotate, skew, or add drop shadows and outlines.
  • Place the dark-circle badge on a dark field, or the sand-circle badge on a light field.
  • Rebuild the wordmark in a different typeface.

Working pairings

Oxford Blue + Soft Sand
Primary dark section. High contrast, calm.
Deep Teal + White
Buttons and confident blocks. Passes AA.
Soft Sand + Charcoal
Default reading surface. Body copy home.
Terracotta + White
Warm accent block. Use in shorter bursts.

Note on contrast: Sunflower Yellow does not pass text-contrast on white or sand. Use it as a fill or highlight, and set text on it in Charcoal, never white.
